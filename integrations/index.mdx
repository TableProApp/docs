AI clients, the Raycast extension and the `tablepro` command query your databases through the
[MCP server](/features/mcp), so its limits, tokens and activity log, all reached from
**Settings > MCP**, apply to them. Opening a link or a file from the terminal, DDEV and iOS Shortcuts
work without it.

  </Card>

    Open a project's database with `ddev tablepro`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shortcuts" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/integrations/shortcuts">
    Run a query, add rows or open a connection from Shortcuts on a Mac, iPhone or iPad.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Help > Integrations** opens the full directory, community integrations included, at [tablepro.app/integrations](https://tablepro.app/integrations).

To build an integration of your own, start at [Developers](/developers).
