Before an imported or synced connection uses this Mac's cloud credentials or Kerberos tickets,
review **Sign In with Credentials from This Mac?**. The dialog names the destination and sign-in
method. Click **Sign In** to approve that use. Links, Linked Folders and Team Library connections
follow the same rule.

Approval stays on this Mac and covers the connection, destination and sign-in settings shown.
A new destination or added credential use asks again. A background reconnect cannot grant approval:
open the connection yourself to review it.
