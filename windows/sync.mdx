TablePro Sync carries your connections, saved queries and favorites through your [TablePro account](/windows/account). Passwords, other secrets and query history are end-to-end encrypted on this computer before they leave it. It needs a plan that includes TablePro Sync.

## Turn on TablePro Sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Choose **Settings** in the **File** menu. **Sync** follows **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose TablePro Sync">
    Set **Sync this computer with** to **TablePro Sync**. Signed out, or on a plan without it, a dialog says what the plan needs, with **Sign in...** or **Buy a license**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what goes up">
    The upload dialog counts the items that go up, the passwords sealed for TablePro Sync, and what stays on this computer: Local only and sample connections, and the categories you turned off. Click **Upload**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first computer on your account sets up a recovery key before anything goes up, and every other computer is approved by one that already syncs. The upload dialog says which one this computer needs.

<Check>
**Status** says **Up to date**, with the time of the last sync and **Sync now**.
</Check>

## What syncs

Expand **What syncs** to choose. The switches apply before the first upload too.

| Switch | What it carries |
|---|---|
| Connections | Every connection not set to Local only, with the SSH and credential profiles they use |
| Passwords | The connections' passwords, end-to-end encrypted. Each computer also keeps its own copy |
| Groups and tags | The groups and tags your connections sit in |
| Saved queries | Saved queries and their folders |
| Table favorites | Marked tables and the table folders around them |
| Database favorites | Marked databases |

Query history syncs end-to-end encrypted, up to the number of entries **History** in Settings keeps. Settings themselves stay on each computer.

## Status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Up to date | The last sync finished. **Sync now** syncs again at once |
| Syncing... | A sync is running |
| Waiting to retry | The last sync failed, and the reason shows under it. It tries again on its own |
| Downloading only | Your plan or your server takes no changes for now; this computer keeps its own until it does |
| Waiting for approval | Approve this computer from another device, or use your recovery key |
| Recovery key needed | Set up your account's recovery key first |
| Locked | This computer can't read your account's keys. The reason and the way back show under it |
| Sync is unavailable | The reason shows under it |

## Signed in to another account

When you sign in to another account, or to another server, nothing goes up until you choose. **Move my data** brings this computer's connections and queries into the new account. **Start empty** takes what the new account already holds instead, and leaves this computer's earlier items with the earlier account.

## Your server's settings

A company server can turn off personal data, passwords or query history. Each one shows as a note under **Sync this computer with**, and what it turns off stays on this computer.

## Turn sync off

Set **Sync this computer with** to **Off**. Your connections and saved queries stay on this computer and stop syncing. Turning it on again uploads what changed meanwhile.
