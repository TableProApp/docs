title: Team Library
description: Open and publish your team's saved queries on Windows, resolve conflicts, and confirm the members who hold the team's keys
---

On Windows, Team Library shares saved queries. It needs a plan with Team Library, such as a seat on a Team plan, and the same [TablePro account](/windows/account) signed in as on the Mac. [Teams](/features/team) covers seats and roles.

## Open the team's queries

Choose **Show saved queries** in the **View** menu. A **Team Library** section under your own queries lists each team's queries, read only, with who changed each one last. Double-click one, or select it and press `Enter`, to open it in a new query tab on the connection the pane belongs to.

## Publish your saved queries

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish to team...">
    It is beside the **Team Library** header. Only owners, admins and editors publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and publish">
    The dialog counts the saved queries on this computer that go to the team's library, with their folders. With several teams, pick one under **Team**. Click **Publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Queries you published before and deleted since leave the team library. A bar under the section says how the publish went. A publish the team library can't take right now stays on this computer, and the bar counts it until it goes out; **Discard...** drops it. [Signing out](/windows/account#sign-out) asks about it first.

When you and a teammate changed the same item, the bar says how many conflict, with **Resolve...**. Each conflict opens in its own dialog, with the item's fields under **Yours** and **The team's** and who changed the team's copy last. Choose **Keep mine**, **Keep theirs** or **Decide later**.

## Team keys

A team shares its passwords end-to-end under a team key. An owner or admin confirms each member by comparing six words before the key goes to that member. Signed in, open Settings and click **Team keys** under **Sync**.

- **Show my words...** shows your six words. Read them to the owner or admin who confirms you. They are the same on every device of your account.
- Each team has a card with its key's status and the action it needs, such as **Create team keys**, **Rotate team key...**, **Compare words...** or **Start over...**.
- Owners and admins also see the members, with **Confirm...** for someone new and **Compare...** for someone whose keys changed, and the devices that stopped using the key, with **Confirm again...**.

To confirm a member, ask them to read the six words under **Show my words...**, click **Confirm...** beside them, and click **Confirm** only if the words match. If they don't, click **Words don't match**: someone may be standing in for that person.

Each member needs [end-to-end keys](/windows/end-to-end-encryption) of their own before an owner or admin can confirm them.

## Not on Windows yet

- **Team connections.** The Mac app publishes connections to Team Library with their passwords. Windows doesn't show them yet, and a publish from Windows sends saved queries only.
