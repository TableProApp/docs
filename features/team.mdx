A team runs from the account portal at [account.tablepro.app](https://account.tablepro.app), and each member signs in to TablePro with an account of their own. A member holding a seat gets Team Catalog and Team Library on their devices, along with everything in Starter.

## Seats

A Team plan is sold per seat, 5 at least, and the team's seats are the sum of the seats on its licenses. A member holds a seat or does not, and only a seat brings the paid features. One seat covers one person on up to 3 Macs. The limit is per account, not per plan, so a seat and a Starter plan on the same account share those 3; see [Devices](/features/licensing#devices).

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| Owner | Everything an admin can, plus billing, transferring the team and deleting it. There is exactly one, and the owner needs no seat |
| Admin | Members, roles and seats, invitations, Team Library publishing, API tokens and the audit log. Nothing that touches the owner |
| Member | Uses the team's features with a seat. Sees the team, its plan, its seat counts, and their own role and seat |

## Manage your team on the web

Sign in at [account.tablepro.app](https://account.tablepro.app) and open the team. Owners and admins invite people by email with a role and, if they choose, a seat, which the invitation reserves until it is accepted or revoked. They change roles and seats and remove members on the same page. The owner changes the number of seats in **Billing**.

In the app, **Teams** in **Settings > Account** lists each team you belong to with your role and **Seat** or **No seat**. **Manage…** opens that team in the portal.

Removing a member, or their seat, takes the team's features off their devices at the next refresh, within a day while they are online. A device that stays offline keeps them until its signed copy expires, 14 days at most.

## Join a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Open the link in the invitation email. It works for 7 days. Sign in, or sign up with the invited address, and click **Accept invitation**. Any TablePro account can accept, and the page says whether the invitation comes with a seat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in on the Mac">
    Sign in to TablePro with the same account. See [Sign in](/features/licensing#sign-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh">
    On a Mac that was signed in already, click **Refresh** in **Settings > Account**. A team joined in the portal reaches the Mac at its next refresh otherwise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>**Teams** lists the team with **Seat**, and **Features** marks Team Catalog and Team Library **Included**.</Check>

Versions before TablePro accounts can no longer join a team with an invite code. Update TablePro, then accept the invitation from its email.

## Share connections with your team

Two routes, and the choice is whether your team already shares a folder. If it does, publish connection definitions into it with [Team Catalog](/features/connection-sharing#team-catalog). If it does not, use Team Library, which keeps the shared set on TablePro's server instead.

### Team Library

Every member with a seat reads the library. Owners and admins publish to it from the app, and delete any item under **Library** in the portal, which lists who last changed each one.

To publish connections, right-click one, choose **Share > Publish to Team Library…**, and click **Publish**. Someone who publishes to several teams picks one from a submenu. Publishing again updates the team's copy.

To publish saved queries, right-click empty space in the Favorites sidebar and choose **Publish Saved Queries to Team…**. Your saved queries go up with their folders, and the ones you published from this Mac and deleted since leave the library. What other people published stays.

Only the connection definition and the query text travel, never passwords, key passphrases or TOTP secrets. The server refuses a publish holding an item over 256 KiB or a field named like a credential, and stores none of it.

TablePro pulls the library at launch, every 15 minutes and right after each publish, and sends only what changed. The **Team Library** header shows a spinner while it syncs and a warning icon after a sync that failed; hover the header for when it last updated. A publish the server cannot take right now says **Not published yet**, stays on the Mac, and goes out once the server answers.

Teammates find the connections in a **Team Library** section of the welcome window, read only, without startup SQL or a tunnel command. Queries land under **Team Library** in the Favorites sidebar, each labeled with who last changed it, or with its folder when the library names no one.

### Library conflicts

A publish that changes an item someone else changed since your last sync leaves a conflict instead. The header shows **1 Conflict**; click it, or choose **Resolve Team Library Conflicts…** from a right-click. The sheet sets **Your Version** beside **Team's Version**, which names who changed it:

- **Keep Mine** replaces the team's version with yours.
- **Keep Theirs** leaves the team's version, and holds until your copy on this Mac changes again.
- **Decide Later** closes the sheet and keeps the conflict.

When the team's plan ends, or your seat goes, Team Library connections and queries disappear from the Mac. The library stays on the server until the owner deletes the team.

Versions before TablePro accounts pull the library at launch, at most once a week, and right after they publish. A publish from one of them replaces what that account published before, and skips any item changed since through a newer version or the portal.
