Joining a team happens in the browser, from an emailed invitation. The app's part is that you are signed in to the account that accepted it. Members, seats, roles and billing all live in your account at [account.tablepro.app](https://account.tablepro.app).

## Roles and seats

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| Owner | Everything an admin can, plus billing, transferring ownership and deleting the team. Exactly one per team, and never removed |
| Admin | Change members' roles and seats, invite and remove members, publish to the team library, manage API tokens |
| Member | Use the team's features and library, and leave the team |

A seat turns the team's paid features on for one person, on up to 3 of their devices; see [Devices](/features/licensing#devices). Someone in the team without a seat keeps a Free plan unless they have one of their own. The owner does not need a seat.

## Manage your team

Sign in at [account.tablepro.app](https://account.tablepro.app) and open the team. **Members** lists who is in it, their role and seat, and the seats taken, such as `3 of 5 seats taken`. Owners and admins work from there:

- **Invite member…** sends an invitation to an email address, with a role and, while a seat is free, **Include a seat**. The seat stays reserved until the invitation is accepted or revoked.
- Pending invitations have **Resend invitation**, which sends a new link, and **Revoke invitation…**, which frees a reserved seat.
- Each member's menu has **Change role**, **Give seat** or **Take seat…**, and **Remove member…**.
- The owner adds seats in **Billing**.

A member whose seat is taken, or who is removed, loses the team's features at their app's next check, within a day while online. On a device that stays offline, they last until its plan file expires, at most 14 days.

In TablePro, **Settings > Account** lists your teams under **Teams**, with your role, **Seat** or **No seat**, and **Manage…**, which opens the team in your account.

## Join a team

## Share connections with your team

Two routes, and the choice is whether your team already shares a folder. If it does, publish connection definitions into it with [Team Catalog](/features/connection-sharing#team-catalog). If it does not, use Team Library, which keeps the shared set in your account instead.

### Team Library

Owners and admins publish: right-click a connection and choose **Share > Publish to Team Library…**, or publish saved queries from the Favorites sidebar with **Publish Saved Queries to Team…**. Only the connection definition and the query text are sent, never passwords, tunnel commands or startup SQL.

Everyone with a seat gets the connections in their list the way a Linked Folder delivers them, and shared queries in the Favorites sidebar under **Team Library**, labeled with whoever published each one. The app pulls the library at launch, every 15 minutes and after each publish. A pulled connection arrives without its startup SQL, because it lands with no confirmation step and nothing that would run by itself travels with it.

Shared sign-in settings need [approval on each Mac](/connections/connection-form#sign-ins-from-this-mac) before using local cloud credentials or Kerberos tickets.

The team's **Library** page in your account lists what was published, and owners and admins delete items there.
